MultiBeam is an open-source fork of Antenna, built to run up to four channels at once.
Channel data & stream links come from the iptv-org/iptv project (Unlicense) — nothing is hosted or stored here.
Public streams can go offline, move, or get geo-blocked without notice, and four at once asks more of your connection than one does.
